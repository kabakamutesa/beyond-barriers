Our Mission

Limits to us are just a suggestion.

Every program begins with the people who live the barrier: students, jobseekers, patients and advocates. They sit at the table before a single lesson plan or clinic route is drawn.

That is the difference between a ramp that meets code and a building people actually want to enter. We work in education, employment, health, advocacy and outreach, and we judge ourselves by what lasts after we leave.

Meet Our Founder

Rose Nyawira, Founder & CEO

“Inclusion is not a favour. It is the starting point of every good design.”

Rose Nyawira founded Beyond Barriers in 2025 after seeing how many capable people in Kenya were shut out of school, work and care by barriers that never had to exist.

Today they lead a team that builds every program alongside the people it serves, and they remain committed to one simple test: does life get better for people with disabilities once we have gone?

What We Do

Five programs, one goal.

Inclusive Education

Accessible classrooms, assistive-tech training and scholarships that let students with disabilities chase their dreams. 8,500+ students and counting.

"For the first time, my daughter has a desk and a screen reader built for her, not adapted for her." — Parent, Kisumu STEM Academy
Learn more

Skills & Employment

Vocational training, job placement and partnerships with inclusive employers. 3,200+ people now work with dignity and a fair wage.

"Beyond Barriers didn't just give me skills — they gave me confidence. Today I run my own business and employ three other people with disabilities." — James M., Program Graduate
Learn more

Health & Wellness

Accessible care, rehabilitation and mental health support. Our mobile clinics have reached 12,000+ patients in rural communities.

Learn more

Advocacy & Rights

Campaigns, legal aid and public awareness that have already won 15 policy reforms.

Community Outreach

Home visits, community centres and mobile services that reach people where they live.
